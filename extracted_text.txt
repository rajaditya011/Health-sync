oConsultation
oMedication
oRecovery planning

--- Page 3 ---
Our Vision
To build an AI -assisted healthcare system that simplifies the entire recovery 
journey while keeping doctors in control.
Not replacing doctors — empowering patients & clinicians

--- Page 4 ---
Solution Overview
HealthSync  is an AI -powered healthcare assistant that:
•Scans medical reports using OCR
•Analyzes conditions using AI
•Recommends the most suitable doctor
•Enables secure online consultation
•Records & transcribes conversations (with consent)
•Extracts prescriptions intelligently
•Sends medication reminders
•Creates personalized recovery, diet & exercise plans
One platform. End -to-end care.

System Architecture
Flow Explanation:
•OCR extracts medical data
•NLP identifies condition & severity
•Recommendation engine ranks doctors
•Speech -to-text transcribes consultation

•Reminder & recovery engines activate
